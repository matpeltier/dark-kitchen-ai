# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want Dark Kitchen AI to be a welcoming, practical project. Participants are expected to be respectful, assume good intent, explain technical disagreements clearly, and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized conduct, threats, and publishing private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer through the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/matpeltier). Reports will be reviewed in confidence where possible. The maintainer may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and constructive.
